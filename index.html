Overview

This page is the web version of the CGCT Model 1 (Collaboration in Content Development) report for MEQ545 Engineering Economics. The developed content is the MEQ545 All-Quiz Tutorial Portal — a web application delivering 10 tutorial sets (114 questions) with digital attendance and submissions monitoring, developed by the teaching team and co-developed with an industry panel. All usage figures are as at 21 October 2026.
